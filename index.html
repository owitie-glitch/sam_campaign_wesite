Samuel’s commitment

Education and opportunity

Samuel’s education agenda centres on useful information, mentorship, skills, and stronger opportunities for young people.

Why it matters

Young people need clearer pathways from learning to useful skills, employment, enterprise, and informed citizenship.

How Samuel intends to act
  • Support mentorship and career-guidance networks
  • Promote practical and digital-skills initiatives
  • Encourage civic education and informed participation
  • Connect learning with local enterprise opportunities
  • Advocate for inclusive access to learning resources
What progress should look like

More residents accessing useful information, mentorship, skills, and pathways to opportunity.
